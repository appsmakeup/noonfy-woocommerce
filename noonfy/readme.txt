Email marketing, automation and multi-provider SMTP delivery for WooCommerce stores.

== Description ==

Adds an **AI Customer Engagement** menu to wp-admin with the SMTP & Email Delivery screen: dashboard, connections, smart routing, queue & logs, domains, deliverability, suppressions and monitoring.

This is an early build. Provider connections, metrics and logs show sample data. Settings (provider toggles, routing strategy and rules, alert rules, log retention, default sender) are saved, and "Send test email" really sends a message through `wp_mail()`.

Requires WooCommerce.
